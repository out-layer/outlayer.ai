How It Works

The Agent Gets Buttons.
Not Keys.

Three things make it possible. Each one is something you can check.

01

You lock the key. You lend it.

You lock your bank login with your own account, the way crypto is locked. Then you lend it to your agent, for as long as you choose. Take it back in one step.

02

The agent runs one approved program

It has a few actions: pay an invoice, send an invoice, check the balance — inside your limits. There is no “show me the key” action. It doesn't exist in the program.

03

Intel's hardware proves it

How do you know it ran that exact program? Every run is signed by the Intel chip it ran on: which code, what went in, what came out. You check it yourself.

Nothing to steal. Nothing to misuse. Proof every time.
