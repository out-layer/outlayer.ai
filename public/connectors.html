How It Works

The Agent Asks.
The Enclave Decides.

Your rules sit where the AI can't reach them — not in its prompt, not in its memory.

01

You connect and set the rules

Connect an account once and write the rules: how much per order, who may receive email, which repositories. They are stored encrypted; only the enclave can open them.

02

Your agent asks

The agent calls a connector by name — place an order, send an email, open a pull request. It never receives your password, token or key.

03

The enclave decides

Inside Intel TDX hardware the connector checks your rules, uses the credential and hands back only the result. Every run comes with an Intel attestation anyone can check.

What Your Agent Can Do.
What You Control.

Five connectors on NEAR mainnet. Each can reach only the service it was built for.

Hyperliquid

Trade perpetual futures
Your agent can
  • Open and close positions
  • Check markets, orders and positions
  • Move funds in and back out
You control
  • Max per order and per position
  • Max leverage and which coins
  • Daily volume, and where withdrawals may go

Polymarket

Trade prediction markets
Your agent can
  • Place and cancel orders
  • Check positions and markets
  • Fund trading and withdraw
You control
  • Max per order and open exposure
  • Daily volume
  • Whether deposits and withdrawals are allowed

Not available in every country — check Polymarket's terms first.

Gmail

Send email from your address
Your agent can
  • Send messages as you
  • Nothing else: it cannot read your inbox
You control
  • Allowed recipients or domains
  • Messages per day
  • A subject prefix on every message

GitHub

Work on your repositories
Your agent can
  • Read code, open issues and pull requests
  • Comment and review
  • Create gists
You control
  • Which repositories
  • Writes per day
  • Whether it may merge or approve, and a marker on everything it writes

Mercury

Pay from a business bank account
Your agent can
  • Pay and issue invoices
  • Watch the ledger
You control
  • Max per payment and a 30-day budget
  • Allowed recipients and payment methods
  • Mercury's own approvals still apply on top
Why You Can Let It

Nothing to Leak.
Nothing to Talk Around.

The agent never holds a key

Passwords, tokens and signing keys are opened only inside the enclave. A hacked or confused agent has nothing to hand over.

Your limits aren't in the prompt

They are checked by the connector itself, outside the AI. No clever wording, no injected instruction changes them — only you can.

Every run can be checked

Each call comes with an Intel attestation of the enclave that ran it. You don't have to take our word for it.

Pay only for what it does

Each operation has a price in USD. No subscription to start.
